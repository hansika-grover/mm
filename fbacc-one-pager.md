# fbacc Dashboard — Plain-English One-Pager

**One line:** Mission control for all our Facebook ad accounts — one screen to see everything and
manage everyone, instead of logging into 50 separate browsers.

**The problem it fixes:** Our Facebook assets are scattered across dozens of separate browser logins
on different computers. To check or change anything, someone opens each one by hand — slow, easy to
lose track, no full picture.

**What it does:**
1. **Shows everything in one place** — every profile, Business Manager, ad account, page, pixel; each
   account's spend, limit, balance, and whether it's live or banned.
2. **Manage from one screen** — give/remove a teammate's access, share or move an account/page/pixel
   from one place to another, create new ones — no opening each browser.
3. **Reverse lookup** — pick any asset, instantly see where it lives and what it connects to
   (which profiles/BMs an account is in; which accounts a pixel is shared to).
4. **Warns on changes** — account disabled, access changed, spend spike → instant alert.
5. **Always fresh** — re-checks in the background; shows how recent each number is (green/yellow/red).
6. **Whole team** — every computer running these accounts reports in automatically.

**The safe part:** Only touches accounts we already own. Can't steal logins, never sends passwords
anywhere, every important action asks for confirmation and is logged.

**Shortest version:** "See all our Facebook accounts, manage everyone's access, and get warned the
moment something breaks — from one dashboard."
